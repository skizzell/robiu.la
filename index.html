How I work

“Every send is a signal.”

Timing, channel, frequency, response. Read those right and you can wake a dead lead without adding noise.

There’s no one-size-fits-all kit. Lock the ICP. Rank by fit and intent. Map the buyer and the champion. Then run personalized outreach and reuse what already converts.

I measure pipeline, conversion, cycle time, and revenue, and I put the budget where the proof is strongest.
